## Morning Edition

_8:00 AM ET_

The post-Black Friday weekend opens with a golden week in Fortnite, a big change to how EA's Skate rolls out content, a stadium-sized streamer event in Tokyo, a GTA-inspired game pitch from the producer of India's Ramayana films, and a Pokémon GO event to put on the calendar.

### Fortnite's Golden Week starts today with boosted treasure odds

Epic says Fortnite's Golden Week begins at 9 AM ET on Saturday, November 28, and runs until the end-of-chapter event on December 5. Every Gold Outfit, vehicle, Back Bling, Pickaxe, Jam Track, and Loading Screen from earlier captain weeks returns to chests, along with the new Solid Gold Captain X Outfit. Epic puts the odds of finding any treasure in a chest at 1 in 21, up from 1 in 62, and doubles the base drop rate for Gold Outfits all week. Treasure Hours today add 15x Gold Outfit odds, 10x vehicle and decal odds, and 1.5x Pirate Shop Access.

- [Fortnite Communities (Epic Games)](https://communities.epicgames.com/thread/golden-week-starts-november-28/MuNA)

### Skate drops seasons and season passes

Developer Full Circle is ending Skate's seasonal structure, PC Gamer reports. Season 5, which ends on December 1, will be the last, and the game will move to what the studio calls "regular, agile content updates." The skate.Pass, skate.Premium Pass, and Tix are going away; unused Tix will be auto-exchanged for items on your track at the end of Season 5, and any left over will be wiped. Skate remains in early access, more than a year after its September 2025 launch.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/sports/skate-is-dropping-seasons-and-season-passes-in-favor-of-regular-agile-content-updates/)

### Streamer Hyper Game Tournament FINAL opens at the Tokyo Dome

OPENREC's two-day "配信者ハイパーゲーム大会 FINAL" (Streamer Hyper Game Tournament FINAL) began at the Tokyo Dome on November 28 and continues on November 29. Sanspo reports that teams led by k4sen, SHAKA, Mokou, and Yuta Seki took the stage in front of about 50,000 spectators, with Junichi Kato opening the show as "Mister Haige." Day two includes Power Pros 2026-2027 with former pro baseball players on commentary and as pinch hitters.

- [Sanspo](https://www.sanspo.com/article/20261128-KC4AL65HWFGKXNWIK5FBLZMMPE/)
- [OPENREC](https://openrec.co.jp/news/098486253)

### Ramayana producer says a "GTA-style" game is in production

Namit Malhotra, producer of the Ramayana films, told Doordarshan that a video game based on the Ramayana is in production and compared his ambitions for it to Grand Theft Auto, according to The Indian Express and NDTV. He said players will be able to "fly with Jatayu and also battle Ravana," and that he will share more "when the time is right." No developer, platforms, title, or release date have been announced.

- [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/bollywood/ramayana-video-game-gta-namit-malhotra-franchise-plans-10982202/)
- [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/feature/gta-style-video-game-based-on-ramayana-in-active-production-reveals-namit-malhotra-12388356)

### Pokémon GO's Pokémon Champions Celebration runs December 8–14

The Pokémon Company says Pokémon GO's Pokémon Champions Celebration runs from 10:00 a.m. on Tuesday, December 8, to 8:00 p.m. on Monday, December 14, local time. Look for Charmander, Starly, and Grookey in the wild, a chance at Absol and Gible, and five-star raids featuring Latias, Latios, Heatran, Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion, Regieleki, and Regidrago. Event Field Research rewards Rare Candy, and a GO Pass Deluxe upgrade costs US$4.99.
