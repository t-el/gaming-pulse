## Morning Edition

_8:00 AM ET_

Black Friday morning arrives with a union vote at Rockstar, fresh UK retail figures for Grand Theft Auto 6, a Witcher 3 Remastered patch, the first look at December's Game Pass wave, and a stack of storefront discounts.

### Rockstar's UK staff vote against IWGB recognition

Rockstar Games employees in the United Kingdom have voted against a formal recognition agreement with the IWGB Game Workers Union, days after Grand Theft Auto 6's launch. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/rockstar-game-workers-union-vote-gta-6/) reports a 75% to 25% result against the deal, with a 93.2% turnout among 1,544 staff at the company's offices in Edinburgh, Leeds, Lincoln, Dundee, and London. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/rockstar-games-has-a-gta-6-hit-on-its-hands-but-its-union-vote-is-a-loss/) likewise reports roughly 75% voting no, and says a yes vote would have required Rockstar management to bargain collectively on pay, hours, and conditions. Both outlets note that an employment tribunal over 2025 firings — Polygon puts the number at 31 workers — is still awaiting a ruling, and that the union can continue to represent members even without formal recognition.

- [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/rockstar-game-workers-union-vote-gta-6/)
- [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/rockstar-games-has-a-gta-6-hit-on-its-hands-but-its-union-vote-is-a-loss/)

### GTA 6 lifts UK boxed sales to an estimated £32 million

[NielsenIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2026/gta-vi-sends-uk-games-retail-sales-soaring-99-to-142m-in-launch-week/) estimates that Grand Theft Auto 6 accounted for £32 million in UK physical software sales during its three-day launch window of November 19–21, around 74% of that week's boxed software revenue. UK games retail as a whole reached £142 million in launch week, up 99% week on week, while boxed software hit an estimated £43 million — a 409% jump and the biggest week for physical games since Christmas 2018. The figures include code-in-a-box copies and exclude digital storefronts such as PlayStation Store and Xbox Store; NielsenIQ ranks the launch No. 17 for first-week boxed revenue among games released since 1995. Hardware rose with it: PlayStation 5 sales were up 46% week on week, and Xbox Series sales jumped 75%. [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/tech/gta-6-sales-numbers-so-far-b3092381.html), citing the same data, notes that the physical edition is a box with a download code rather than a disc.

- [NielsenIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2026/gta-vi-sends-uk-games-retail-sales-soaring-99-to-142m-in-launch-week/)
- [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/tech/gta-6-sales-numbers-so-far-b3092381.html)

### Witcher 3 Remastered patch 5.02 fixes werewolves, lighting, and a Gwent card

Patch 5.02 is out for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/the-witcher/witcher-3-remastered-patch-has-lighting-fixes-sign-nerfs-and-gets-rid-of-a-nasty-werewolf-bug/) reports that the notes fix werewolves regenerating health too quickly on higher difficulties, "making them impossible to defeat," along with lighting that was too dark in interiors and blown out in other locations and cutscenes. The same patch walks back some sign power, including a 10% cut to the Catalyst skill's bonus to Aard and Igni intensity, and places Zoltan Chivay's Gwent card under the Hanged Man's Tree in Velen so collectors can find it.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/the-witcher/witcher-3-remastered-patch-has-lighting-fixes-sign-nerfs-and-gets-rid-of-a-nasty-werewolf-bug/)

### Xbox Game Pass names its first December arrivals

[GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-game-pass-kicks-off-december-with-horror-cozy-adventures-and-more/) has the first half of December's Xbox Game Pass wave, and notes the list is not complete yet. On December 1, The Outlast Trials arrives on cloud, Xbox Series X|S, and PC for Game Pass Ultimate, Game Pass Premium, and PC Game Pass, while Hela: Of Mice and Magic lands as a day-one addition on cloud, Xbox Series X|S, and PC for Ultimate and PC Game Pass. GameSpot also lists World of Tanks Blitz for that day, then Freestyle Football 2 on December 3 and the roguelite Nubby's Number Factory on December 14.

- [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/xbox-game-pass-kicks-off-december-with-horror-cozy-adventures-and-more/)

### Black Friday deals include a Switch 2 bundle and steep game discounts

[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/best-black-friday-2026-gaming-deals/) published its Black Friday gaming roundup this morning, highlighting a discounted Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle that, the outlet says, essentially throws in that game plus three months of Nintendo Switch Online. Those tags can move through the weekend, so confirm the price at the retailer before you buy.

- [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/best-black-friday-2026-gaming-deals/)

### Marvelous offers voluntary exits as it aims to cut 65 console-division jobs

[Video Games Chronicle](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/story-of-seasons-and-rune-factory-developer-marvelous-is-offering-early-retirement-as-it-tries-to-cut-65-jobs/) reports that Marvelous, the Japanese studio behind Story of Seasons and Rune Factory, is accepting applications for voluntary resignations from December 14, 2026, to January 8, 2027, as it looks to reduce its Consumer Business Division by 65 employees. Those who apply are expected to leave by March 31, 2027, and will receive a special lump-sum payment plus optional help finding other work. The company says it wants to lean more on external partners as projects get larger and cycles get longer, while stressing that console games remain a core business.

- [Video Games Chronicle](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/story-of-seasons-and-rune-factory-developer-marvelous-is-offering-early-retirement-as-it-tries-to-cut-65-jobs/)

## Afternoon Edition

_2:00 PM ET_

The afternoon brings a release date for Hollow Knight: Silksong's first expansion, Rockstar North's take on GTA 6's most debated system, public mod tools for Vermintide 2, and a surprising Fortnite crossover.

### Hollow Knight: Silksong's free Sea of Sorrow expansion arrives December 11

Team Cherry used its Cherry Time livestream to give a first look at Sea of Sorrow, the first major expansion for Hollow Knight: Silksong. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/hollow-knight-silksongs-first-major-expansion-is-a-free-trip-under-the-sea/) reports that the expansion is free, launches December 11, and takes players to a new aquatic region with new areas, tools, characters, items, and bosses. The same stream also showed three other games: Blokkan: Secret of the Walking City, Radio Rain, and The Botanist and the Translator.

- [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/hollow-knight-silksongs-first-major-expansion-is-a-free-trip-under-the-sea/)

### Rockstar North's boss says GTA 6's criminal profiles add options, not limits

GTA 6's criminal profile system, which tracks how violently you play as Jason and Lucia, has split players. [IGN](https://sea.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-rumored-title/252512/rockstar-north-boss-knew-some-gta-6-players-would-reject-new-systems-like-criminal-profiles-but-insi) highlights comments that Rockstar co-studio head Rob Nelson gave to VGC, where he said the system "changes what GTA can be" and that "you can still do whatever you wanted to do before, but now you have other options." IGN notes that the profile you end up with factors into which ending you get, and that Nelson expects some players to "reject this new stuff we're offering."

- [IGN](https://sea.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-rumored-title/252512/rockstar-north-boss-knew-some-gta-6-players-would-reject-new-systems-like-criminal-profiles-but-insi)

### Vermintide 2 opens its developer tools to modders

Fatshark has publicly released Vermintide 2's internal developer tools so modders can create custom levels, models, textures, particles, and animations, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/vermintide-2-mod-tools-get-a-public-release-and-the-games-90-percent-off-on-steam-too/) reports. An early-December update will give the Modded Realm its own local, offline progression. At the same time, the dedicated Versus Mode servers will shut down, though Versus stays playable through custom games and the lobby browser. The game is 90% off on Steam, and XP is doubled through Monday. Fatshark also says the previously mentioned weapon pack and level are on hold.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/vermintide-2-mod-tools-get-a-public-release-and-the-games-90-percent-off-on-steam-too/)

### Doki Doki Literature Club joins Fortnite on November 28

Monika, Sayori, Natsuki, and Yuri are coming to Fortnite's Item Shop in a Doki Doki Literature Club collaboration that starts November 28, according to [IGN](https://in.ign.com/doki-doki-literature-club/278043/doki-doki-literature-clubs-monika-sayori-natsuki-and-yuri-are-coming-to-fortnite-in-new-crossover). The four outfits keep the characters' school uniforms, hairstyles, and accessories, and each comes with a custom lobby pose.

- [IGN](https://in.ign.com/doki-doki-literature-club/278043/doki-doki-literature-clubs-monika-sayori-natsuki-and-yuri-are-coming-to-fortnite-in-new-crossover)
