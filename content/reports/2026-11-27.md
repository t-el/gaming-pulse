## Morning Edition

_8:00 AM ET_

Friday opens with a remaster patch, a Warhammer bargain, and storefronts dressed for Black Friday.

### Witcher 3 Remastered walks back a werewolf bug and some sign power

Patch 5.02 is out for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/the-witcher/witcher-3-remastered-patch-has-lighting-fixes-sign-nerfs-and-gets-rid-of-a-nasty-werewolf-bug/) says werewolves on higher difficulties were regenerating health so fast they could not be beaten, and that lighting in interiors and cutscenes was swinging between too dark and blown out. The same piece notes CD Projekt pulling back some sign and mutation strength, including a smaller bonus from the Catalyst skill, plus a fix so Zoltan Chivay's Gwent card shows up under the Hanged Man's Tree. [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/the-witcher-3-patch-40-mods-bugs) also reports the patch adding a large batch of cross-platform mods and cleaning up game-breaking bugs.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/the-witcher/witcher-3-remastered-patch-has-lighting-fixes-sign-nerfs-and-gets-rid-of-a-nasty-werewolf-bug/)
- [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/the-witcher-3-patch-40-mods-bugs)

### Vermintide 2 ships public mod tools, and a steep Steam discount

Fatshark's co-op shooter is having a tools moment. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/vermintide-2-mod-tools-get-a-public-release-and-the-games-90-percent-off-on-steam-too/) reports a public release of the Vermintide 2 mod tools alongside a 90 percent Steam discount. If you bounced off the game at full price, this is the weekend the barrier is mostly time.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/vermintide-2-mod-tools-get-a-public-release-and-the-games-90-percent-off-on-steam-too/)

### Steam's Black Friday page is not a formal fest, and it is still full of discounts

Valve is not running an official Black Friday event, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/steam-isnt-doing-an-official-black-friday-fest-but-it-does-have-a-page-with-hundreds-of-discounts-and-black-friday-splashed-across-the-top-so-you-might-want-to-check-it-out-anyway/) notes, but the store does have a page with hundreds of discounts and the words Black Friday across the top. Treat the prices as a moving target and check the cart before you assume a deal is still live.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/steam-isnt-doing-an-official-black-friday-fest-but-it-does-have-a-page-with-hundreds-of-discounts-and-black-friday-splashed-across-the-top-so-you-might-want-to-check-it-out-anyway/)

### Marvelous opens a voluntary redundancy scheme

Away from the sales banners, [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/marvelous-developer-volentary-redundancy-amidst-console-business-restructuring) reports that Marvelous, the Story of Seasons studio, has opened a voluntary redundancy scheme as it looks to cut 65 roles during a console-business restructure. It is a jobs story sitting next to a holiday sales cycle, which is the shape of a lot of Friday mornings in this industry.

- [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/marvelous-developer-volentary-redundancy-amidst-console-business-restructuring)

## Afternoon Edition

_2:00 PM ET_

The middle briefing is about how games get updated once the launch trailer is over: seasons, DLC, and campaign structure.

### EA ends Skate's seasonal structure

EA is pulling some of the live-service calendar off Skate. [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/ea-removes-live-service-skate-seasons) reports the studio is dropping the seasonal structure in favor of smaller, more frequent updates. For a reboot that has lived inside a battle-pass rhythm, that is a meaningful change in how new spots and gear show up.

- [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/ea-removes-live-service-skate-seasons)

### The Sims 4 makes a stack of DLC free for a limited time

[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/the-sims-4-free-maker-pack-holiday-giveaway-2026/) reports that The Sims 4 is making 13 DLCs free for a limited holiday window. The giveaway is the practical headline: if a pack you skipped is on the list, grab it while the timer is running, then decide later whether it earns a place in the load order.

- [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/the-sims-4-free-maker-pack-holiday-giveaway-2026/)

### Dawn of War 4 campaigns fork with the commander you pick

Relic's next Warhammer 40,000 RTS is describing a campaign that does not play the same for every faction. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rts/dawn-of-war-4s-campaigns-differ-based-on-your-choices-which-character-you-choose-and-what-decisions-you-make-with-them-lead-down-different-experiences-of-an-overlapping-story/) quotes the pitch that the character you choose, and the decisions you make with them, lead through different experiences of an overlapping story. That is the kind of claim a campaign preview has to earn in the missions, but it is the note the studio wants in the conversation today.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rts/dawn-of-war-4s-campaigns-differ-based-on-your-choices-which-character-you-choose-and-what-decisions-you-make-with-them-lead-down-different-experiences-of-an-overlapping-story/)

### Switch 2 Black Friday coverage is a deals roundup, not a new console

[IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deals-2026) is running a Switch 2 Black Friday guide covering consoles, games, and memory cards, alongside a wider UK deals piece and prebuilt-PC roundups. Prices in those guides move during the weekend. Use them as a map, then confirm the tag at the retailer before you buy.

- [IGN: Switch 2 Black Friday deals](https://www.ign.com/articles/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deals-2026)

## Evening Edition

_8:00 PM ET_

The late briefing stays with Grand Theft Auto 6, the studio that made it, and a craft argument from Team Ninja.

### GTA 6 is still the sales and guide story

A week-plus into launch, the coverage has split into how-to hubs and retail charts. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-guides-hub-how-to-do-everything-in-grand-theft-auto-6/) has a guides hub up, which is what a game looks like once players are past the opening hours. On the money side, [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-boxed-physical-sales-uk) reports a surge in UK physical game sales even though the box does not include a disc. The boxed chart and the guide index are the same phenomenon: a lot of people are in the game right now.

- [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-guides-hub-how-to-do-everything-in-grand-theft-auto-6/)
- [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/gta-6-boxed-physical-sales-uk)

### Rockstar UK staff vote against union recognition

[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/rockstar-games-employees-vote-against-union-recognition) reports that Rockstar Games employees voted against union recognition. The vote lands while GTA 6 is the center of the release calendar, which is why it is in the evening edition rather than buried under another deals link. Read the outlet's account for the tally and the responses from the union and the company.

- [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/rockstar-games-employees-vote-against-union-recognition)

### Path of Exile 2's director takes a shot at announcement style

Grinding Gear Games' Jonathan Rogers is quoted by [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blizzard-im-looking-at-you-path-of-exile-2-boss-criticizes-truly-terrible-game-announcements-that-hold-the-juiciest-details-until-the-end/) criticizing game announcements that hold the useful details until the end, with Blizzard named in the headline framing. It is a craft complaint from a studio that sells leagues on the specifics. Worth reading before the next showcase tries the slow roll.

- [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/blizzard-im-looking-at-you-path-of-exile-2-boss-criticizes-truly-terrible-game-announcements-that-hold-the-juiciest-details-until-the-end/)

### Team Ninja says it uses AI for QA, and that fun is still made by hand

[Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/nioh-ai-qa-game-human-hands) reports that the Nioh and Ninja Gaiden studio is using AI for quality assurance, while arguing that a game becomes fun when it is crafted by human hands. That split, tools on the test bench and people on the design, is the version of the AI conversation most studios are actually having.

- [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/nioh-ai-qa-game-human-hands)
